Modular Femoral Endoprosthesis

Advanced modular implant system designed for precision, durability, and optimal patient outcomes in complex joint reconstruction.

Technical Specifications

science

Core Material

Titanium Alloy (Ti-6Al-4V ELI) conforming to ISO 5832-3 standards for superior strength-to-weight ratio and fatigue resistance.

layers

Bio-Coating

Hydroxyapatite (HA) plasma spray over porous titanium coating to facilitate osseointegration and long-term stability.

precision_manufacturing

Taper Junction

Precision-machined 12/14 morse taper ensuring a secure, cold-welded micro-connection between modular components.
